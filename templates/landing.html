Workflows

Built around how trainers actually work.

Six everyday jobs - each one designed to take less than a minute, so admin doesn't eat into your coaching time.

01
Onboard a new client

Capture their goals, preferred training times, and any injuries to work around. From first conversation to plan-ready in under a minute, with nothing to re-key later.

02
Draft a plan with AI

Skip the blank page. The assistant suggests a multi-day program shaped to the client's goals so you start from something thoughtful and shape it before they ever see it.

03
Schedule the week

Block out consultations, training sessions, and check-ins in one place. Cancellations stay visible so your week's true rhythm is honest, not hidden.

04
Log what was done

Capture what really happened in the session, not what was planned. Trends and adherence update automatically so the next program is grounded in evidence.

05

See when a lift is plateauing or attendance is slipping before it becomes a tough conversation. Every metric a client might ask about is one click away.
